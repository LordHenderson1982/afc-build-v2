# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:
- TikTok account credentials (via OAuth, securely managed by Composio)
- Basic account information (username, profile data)
- Content you choose to post through our service

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:
- Provide AI-assisted social media management services
- Post content to your TikTok account
- Retrieve analytics and engagement data
- Improve our services

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- Your credentials are securely stored by Composio
- We do not store your TikTok password
- Data is encrypted in transit

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. Your data is only shared with TikTok as required for the services you request.

## 5. Third-Party Services

This application uses Composio to connect to TikTok. Their privacy policy applies to how they handle your data.

## 6. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the app.
