### Option 3: M3U Playlist

1. **Get your credentials** — Username and password are sent via email after purchase
2. **Download an IPTV app** — Use apps that support M3U:
   - **VLC Media Player** (PC/Mac)
   - **IPTV Extreme** (Android)
3. **Add the M3U URL**:
   ```
   http://svg.pm:25461/get.php?username=YOURUSERNAME&password=YOURPASSWORD&type=m3u_plus&output=mpegts
   ```
   *(Replace YOURUSERNAME and YOURPASSWORD with your actual credentials)*
4. **Login** — Use your username and password

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### ❌ "No Stream Available" / Channel Not Loading

**Solutions:**
1. **Check your internet** — Minimum 15 Mbps recommended
2. **Try a different player** — Some channels work better on certain apps
3. **Check for server maintenance** — Visit our status page
4. **Verify credentials** — Ensure username/password are correct

### ❌ Buffering / Freezing

**Solutions:**
1. **Speed test** — Run a speed test; must be stable
2. **Use wired connection** — Ethernet over WiFi when possible
3. **Change server** — Some servers are less congested
4. **Reduce quality** — Try SD instead of HD/4K
5. **Restart device** — Clear cache and restart

### ❌ Login Failed / Authentication Error

**Solutions:**
1. **Check credentials** — Ensure no extra spaces
2. **Subscription active?** — Contact support if expired
3. **Too many devices?** — Some plans limit connections

### ❌ Audio But No Video (or Vice Versa)

**Solutions:**
1. **Change player** — Try a different app
2. **Update app** — Ensure you're on latest version
3. **Clear cache** — Delete app data and re-login

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long does activation take?
**A:** Activation is automatic and takes 10-24 hours after purchase. You'll receive an email with your login details once ready.

### Q: Can I use on multiple devices?
**A:** Check your plan details. Some plans allow multiple connections. Using more than allowed may result in temporary suspension.

### Q: What internet speed do I need?
**A:** Minimum 15 Mbps for HD, 25+ Mbps for 4K. A stable connection is more important than raw speed.

### Q: Do you offer refunds?
**A:** We offer a 24-hour trial for new customers. Refund policy varies by plan — contact support.

### Q: What if a channel is down?
**A:** Report it to support. We actively monitor and restore channels, but some live sports may have rights restrictions.

### Q: Can I record live TV?
**A:** Recording is not built-in. Use your device's DVR feature if supported, or third-party recording apps.

### Q: Do you offer PPV events?
**A:** Yes! PPV events are included in most plans. Check the PPV categories in your channel list.
