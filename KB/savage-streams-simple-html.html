Setup Instructions

Option 1: Savage TV 4OUR (Recommended for All Our Services)

Savage TV 4OUR is our dedicated app that works with Bones IPTV, Savage Streams, StreamHub IPTV, and Titanium VOD.

  1. Download the Downloader App — From the App Store or Play Store
  2. Install on your device — Enable "Install from unknown sources" in settings
  3. Open the Downloader app — Enter the short code: 727397
  4. Install Savage 4OUR
  5. Open Savage 4OUR — Enter your Username and Password for any of our 4 services

To add multiple services: Click the settings gear in the top right and click "Add Playlist".
To switch between services: Click the settings gear and click "Switch Playlist".

Option 2: Xtream API (Recommended for Smarters Pro, TiviMate, etc.)

  1. Get your credentials — Username and password are sent via email after purchase
  2. Download an IPTV app — Recommended: TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, Perfect Player, Smart IPTV
  3. Enter Xtream API details:
    • Portal URL: http://svg.pm:25461
    • Username: Your username
    • Password: Your password
  4. Start watching

Option 3: M3U Playlist

  1. Get your credentials
  2. Download an IPTV app — VLC Media Player, IPTV Extreme
  3. Add the M3U URL: http://svg.pm:25461/get.php?username=YOURUSERNAME&password=***&type=m3u_plus&output=mpegts
  4. Login with your credentials

Troubleshooting

No Stream Available / Channel Not Loading

  • Check your internet — Minimum 15 Mbps recommended
  • Try a different player
  • Check for server maintenance
  • Verify credentials are correct

Buffering / Freezing

  • Run a speed test — must be stable
  • Use wired connection (Ethernet over WiFi)
  • Change server
  • Reduce quality (try SD instead of HD/4K)
  • Restart device and clear cache

Login Failed / Authentication Error

  • Check credentials — ensure no extra spaces
  • Verify subscription is active
  • Check if too many devices connected
